# Exhaust the Design Space

Use this procedure for a novel interaction or architectural choice with no established solution. It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic.

1. Set the comparison criteria before building the candidates. Use the user's goal and constraints.
2. Build two or three distinct prototypes. Compare different approaches, not cosmetic variations of one approach.
3. Compare the observed results against the same criteria. Record what each prototype proves and what remains untested.
4. Choose the strongest candidate. Give the reasons, tradeoffs, and evidence behind the choice.

Keep ordinary changes with an established solution outside this procedure.
